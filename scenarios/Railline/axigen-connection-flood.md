## Axigen connection flood

Detects a high rate of new connections from one source to Axigen services. The
bucket holds 30 connection events and leaks one every ten seconds.

Confidence is intentionally low because bursts can also be generated by a
legitimate but malfunctioning client. Adjust the threshold locally if normal
traffic is unusually bursty.

Requires the `Railline/axigen-logs` parser.
